2Risk of loss

Trading gold and other leveraged products carries a high level of risk and is not suitable for everyone. You can lose some or all of the money in your account, and with some account types you may owe more than you deposited.

  • Gold can gap, spike and reverse sharply, especially around news, data releases, geopolitical events, market open and close, and low-liquidity hours.
  • Leverage magnifies losses as well as gains.
  • Only trade with money you can afford to lose completely.

3Grid and martingale risk

Gold Pilot is a grid-recovery system. It adds further positions, in increasing sizes, when price moves against an open basket. This can produce frequent small wins and, at the same time, a rare and very large loss.

  • Deeper baskets use larger lot sizes, so losses and margin use grow quickly as rungs are added.
  • Margin calls and broker stop-outs can close positions at a loss before any of the software's own limits are reached.
  • Features such as the max-rungs cap, hard rung cutoff, floating-loss limit, take-profit decay, cooldown and news and session filters are risk controls, not guarantees. They reduce exposure; they do not remove it.
  • These controls can themselves lock in a loss when they trigger, and they depend on the broker executing orders. Slippage, gaps and spread widening can cause a result worse than the limit you set.
