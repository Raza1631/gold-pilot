Under the hood

Every control, in plain English.

Entry & grid
Straddle distance, grid step between rungs, basket take-profit, and slippage tolerance. Distances mean the same real price move on any broker's gold quote.
Variable grid step
Optionally widen the step in up to four depth brackets so deep rungs sit further apart.
Rung governor
Max-rungs cap, TP decay ramp (full TP, slide to breakeven, optional hold, optional negative segment) and an independent hard rung cutoff.
Loss & profit limits
Floating-loss force-close, daily profit cap that pauses new baskets, and a cooldown that only applies after baskets of a depth you choose.
Sessions
Five session windows entered in your own local time with a single offset input, or a 24-hour mode.
News filter
Blocks new baskets before and after high-impact USD events, with the window sizes set by you.
Alerts
Sound and pop-up alerts at deep-rung milestones, and a distinct alert when the hard cutoff fires.
Order handling
Respects each broker's minimum stop distance and retries a rejected straddle leg instead of leaving it half-placed.

Before you buy

Is it safe for a small account?

A small balance means fewer rungs can be carried, so set max rungs, the floating-loss limit and the cutoff to fit the balance. [Add your account-size guidance.] No setting makes trading risk-free.

Will it work on my broker?

It runs on any MT5 broker offering a gold symbol. It has been used on Vantage and Exness. Your licence is tied to your account number and server name.

Can I run it on more than one account?

Yes, on the Trader and Fund Manager licences. Each account is registered to your licence.

What if gold makes an extreme move?

That is the case the cutoff, the floating-loss limit and the decay ramp exist for. They cap how long a basket can stay open, but a fast move can still produce a loss.
