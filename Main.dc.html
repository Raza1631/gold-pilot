The problem with most grid bots

They win small, win often, and then one runaway move eats everything.

Gold does not move politely. A bot that only knows how to add another position will, sooner or later, meet a move it cannot outlast. Gold Pilot was built around that one bad day: it watches the depth of every basket and tightens its own exit as the basket gets deeper, then closes by rule instead of by hope.

The strategy

Four moves. One basket. One exit.

1

Straddle

A buy-stop and a sell-stop sit either side of price. Whichever fires first sets the direction; the other is cancelled.

2

Grid ladder

If price moves against the trade, the next rung is added one grid step away, with lots following a Fibonacci ladder.

3

One shared TP

Every rung shares one take-profit, recalculated from the basket's weighted-average entry as rungs are added.

4

Decay & exit

Deeper baskets get a smaller target, down to breakeven, so recovery needs less and less from the market.

The Fibonacci lot ladder

Each rung's size is the sum of the previous two, so a recovery needs only a small bounce. The depth is capped by you, with a max-rungs limit and a hard cutoff.

Signature feature

The TP decay ramp

Early rungs aim for the full target. As the basket deepens, the target slides to breakeven, can hold there, and can even step below it, so the basket always has a way out. Every breakpoint is a setting you control.

  • ▸Full TP through the rungs you choose
  • ▸Linear slide to breakeven, with an optional hold
  • ▸Optional negative segment for a pure get-out exit
  • ▸Or switch it off and run a flat TP

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is Gold Pilot risk-free?

No. It is a grid and martingale-style system: it can win often and still take a large loss on an extreme move. The built-in limits reduce that risk but cannot remove it. Only trade money you can afford to lose.

What platform and account do I need?

MetaTrader 5 on a gold (XAUUSD) symbol. It has been run on cent and standard accounts at Vantage and Exness. A VPS is recommended so it runs around the clock. [Confirm minimum balance and leverage guidance.]

How does the licence work?

Each copy is locked to the MT5 account (and broker server) you register, with an expiry date for time-limited plans. If an expiry passes, it stops opening new baskets and manages any open basket to its close.

Can I change the settings?

Yes. Distances, rung limits, the TP decay ramp, hard cutoff, sessions, cooldown and news filter are all inputs. [Describe the preset files you supply.]

What happens during big news?

The news filter blocks new baskets around high-impact USD events. Baskets already open continue to be managed by the rules you set.

Do you offer refunds?

No. Gold Pilot is a digital product that is licensed to your account on purchase, so all sales are final with no refunds or returns. Please read the refund policy and disclaimer before you buy.
